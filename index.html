MORAL SWITCH

The trolley problem, put to Jev — a decision model that answers with probabilities, not essays.

Bring your own Pollen: you approve the budget on the consent screen, and every verdict bills to your balance. This app never sees or stores a secret key.

How it works. Pick a scenario — or write your own. Pull the lever (or don't). Between your finger and the lever stands Jev (typesafe/jev-1.13 via POST /alpha/decisions), which judges the situation three ways at once: a choice with probabilities, a yes/no conscience, and a 0–100 moral score. You see how sure it is, and whether it flips when the options are reversed.
